Why Dengue Risk Peaks Every Monsoon in Karachi

Karachi's monsoon season — June through September — creates ideal conditions for the Aedes aegypti mosquito, the primary vector of dengue fever. As rainfall increases, small collections of standing water form everywhere: coolers, plant pots, blocked drains, roof puddles, and even bottle caps. Each is a potential breeding site.

Dengue is not the only concern. Anopheles mosquitoes — which carry malaria — also proliferate in the same season. Even non-disease-carrying mosquitoes disrupt sleep and daily life, and their presence in homes with young children or elderly family members is a persistent health stressor.

Machar ka ilaj — mosquito treatment — is most effective when done proactively, before a dengue case is detected in your neighbourhood. Waiting until after the bite means the cycle is already underway.

The challenge with DIY mosquito sprays and coils is that they suppress adult mosquitoes temporarily but do nothing to interrupt the breeding cycle in standing water. A professional treatment combines both dimensions: fast knockdown of adult mosquitoes plus larvicide that prevents the next generation from hatching.
