## Urdu and Roman Urdu pest names (for bilingual questions)

- Khatmal (کھٹمل, also spelled khutmal) = bed bug (Cimex lectularius). Treatment is a two-session protocol: residual spray plus insect growth regulator, follow-up visit on day 10 to 14, priced by property size from the bed bug tier in the table above, with a 90-day guarantee. Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/khatmal-ka-ilaj-karachi
- Deemak or dimak (دیمک) = termite. Treatment is drill-and-inject soil treatment plus wood treatment, priced from the termite tier above. Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/deemak-ka-ilaj-karachi
- Lal beg (لال بیگ) = cockroach. General fumigation (gel bait plus residual spray, one visit) covers cockroaches, ants and crawling insects, priced from the fumigation tier above. Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/cockroach-killer-spray-karachi
- Chipkali = house gecko (lizard). Repellents and home remedies do not last; the fix is insect control (removing the gecko's food) plus sealing entry gaps. Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/chipkali-bhagane-ka-tarika
- Machar = mosquito. Dengue season in Karachi runs September to November; indoor and outdoor spray is priced from the mosquito tier above.
