Pest Control in Saddar Karachi

Saddar is Karachi's oldest commercial core, dating to the colonial era, with a dense concentration of old multi-story buildings, wholesale markets around Empress Market, and narrow lanes running between them. Decades-old masonry and timber-frame construction here is consistently among the city's highest-risk zones for both termites and cockroaches — the buildings have simply had more decades to develop the cracks, cavities and structural gaps that pests exploit.

Saddar's ground-floor wholesale and retail shops typically share storage godowns above and behind them with any residential units in the same buildings. That shared storage space is ideal rodent harborage, and because the boundary between commercial and residential floors in Saddar's older buildings is often just an internal staircase, rodent and cockroach activity moves freely between the two.

Our approach in Saddar starts with a structural assessment of the building — roof beams, window frames, shared risers and storage areas — before any chemical is applied. For confirmed termite infestations we use a soil injection and bait station combination with a 3-year structural guarantee. For mixed commercial-residential buildings, we treat storage and shop-floor harborage points alongside the residential units, not just the flat that called us.

General fumigation for a Saddar unit up to 100 sq yards starts from Rs 7,000. For ground-floor shops and godowns, we offer a dedicated commercial programme — see our commercial service.

Pest Control in Civil Lines Karachi

Civil Lines is a colonial-era planned district adjoining Saddar, home to older government and institutional buildings alongside residential bungalows. Like Saddar itself, Civil Lines' housing stock is old enough that original cement rendering and masonry have had decades to crack and develop the cavities that give termites and cockroaches an entry point.

The termite risk profile in Civil Lines' aging structures is comparable to DHA Phase 1's oldest 1960s bungalows — unaddressed cracks and cavities that have gone unattended for decades are the common thread. Institutional buildings mixed in with residential bungalows can also mean inconsistent maintenance history block to block, so pest pressure varies more within Civil Lines than in a uniformly built residential scheme.

Our treatment in Civil Lines begins the same way it does across Karachi's older neighbourhoods: a structural assessment of roof beams, window frames and skirting boards, followed by soil injection and bait station treatment for confirmed termite infestations, backed by a 3-year structural guarantee. For institutional or mixed-use buildings, we can schedule a full-building assessment rather than treating unit by unit.

General fumigation starts from Rs 7,000 for homes up to 100 sq yards, and termite treatment from Rs 13,000. Book an inspection if your Civil Lines property hasn't had a structural pest check in several years.

Pest Control in Garden East & Garden West Karachi

Garden East and Garden West are among Karachi's oldest residential settlements, sitting between the Lyari River and Nishtar Road, adjoining Karachi Zoo. Much of the housing stock here is colonial and early-modernist, dating to the 1950s or earlier, with old timber roofing and window frames that carry a termite exposure comparable to Karachi's other 1950s-era neighbourhoods.

The area's proximity to the Lyari River corridor is the second major pest driver. That location puts Garden East and West in the same standing-water and drainage-overflow risk category documented along the Gujjar Nala/Lyari system further inland — monsoon rain that collects near the riverbank and in poorly drained lanes is a reliable mosquito-breeding trigger, on top of the area's aging-construction termite risk.

Our fumigation visits in Garden East and West apply larvicide to standing water points as a standard part of the service rather than as an add-on, alongside residual insecticide on exterior walls. For termite concerns in the area's older bungalows, we follow the same structural-assessment-first approach used across Karachi's other 1950s-era housing stock, with a 3-year structural guarantee on confirmed infestations.

General fumigation with larvicide included starts from Rs 7,000 for homes up to 100 sq yards, and termite treatment from Rs 13,000.

Pest Control in Soldier Bazaar Karachi

Soldier Bazaar is a dense historic food and retail bazaar centred on Clayton Road, established in the 1850s, with the current market building dating to 1954 — one of Karachi's oldest continuously operating food markets. That history means decades of accumulated grease, food waste, and storage areas throughout the bazaar, which sustains large, entrenched cockroach populations that a single treatment cannot fully eliminate on its own.

Bazaar-adjacent residential lanes in Soldier Bazaar share boundary walls and drainage with the market itself. Pest pressure from the bazaar routinely spills into nearby homes — the same dynamic documented on DHA Phase 2's Khayaban-e-Ittehad commercial strip, where a residential-only treatment that ignores the shared commercial source sees reinfestation within weeks.

Our Soldier Bazaar protocol includes drainage cover treatment and perimeter gel baiting at shared re-entry points between market stalls and adjoining residences, not interior spraying alone. For bazaar shops themselves, we offer a dedicated commercial programme with monthly visits, WHO-approved chemicals safe for food-preparation environments, and documentation for health inspections.

Rodent control in Soldier Bazaar starts from Rs 13,000 for homes up to 100 sq yards, with commercial rates available for bazaar shops on request. See our commercial service.

Pest Control on Burns Road Karachi

Burns Road is Karachi's most famous food street, in continuous operation since the late 1940s post-Partition era. Extremely high food-stall density and grease-heavy cooking all along the strip create sustained, high-volume cockroach and rodent pressure affecting every building along the road and its side lanes — not just the food stalls themselves.

Because Burns Road's food outlets sit directly beside residential and mixed-use buildings, pest colonies established in shop kitchens and storage areas routinely migrate into adjoining floors through shared drainage and utility ducts. A residential unit treated in isolation on Burns Road will see reinfestation quickly if the source pressure from neighbouring food stalls isn't addressed at the same time.

Our approach on Burns Road combines drainage cover treatment and perimeter gel baiting at shop-to-residence boundary points with tamper-resistant rodent bait stations placed at utility entry points, cutting off the shared drains that carry pest pressure between the commercial strip and residential floors above.

General fumigation starts from Rs 7,000 for residential units up to 100 sq yards; commercial rates for offices and shops are quoted based on unit size. Contact us for a commercial quote.
