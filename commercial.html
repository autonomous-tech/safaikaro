Flagship offer

The Full Building Package: Har Floor, Har Zone, Ek Hi Engagement

Most pest control vendors quote you a floor. A building is not a floor. Cockroaches move up service ducts, rats move down from the roof tank housing and up from the basement, and treating the third floor alone just relocates the problem. The Full Building Package treats the structure as one job: every floor, the basement, the stairwells and shafts, and the roof, on a single mobilisation and a single schedule.

Zones we treat

Basement Parking, generator and pump rooms, storage. Damp corners and drain openings where rodents and cockroaches nest.
Every floor Floor by floor treatment of pantries, washrooms, false ceilings, cable trays and store rooms, whatever the tenant mix.
Stairwells and shafts Service ducts, lift shafts and vertical pipe runs. These are the highways between floors, so they get treated as their own zone.
Roof Water tank surrounds, parapets and roof access points. Larvicide where standing water collects, sealing advice where rodents get in.
Drains and entry points Kitchen and washroom drains, external gullies, garbage points and the ground floor perimeter.
Finish and handover Optional plastering and finish work after treatment access, so the building is presentable on the day you hand it over.

What the package includes

  • Full structure fumigation, zone by zone, with the schedule written down before we start
  • Cockroach, rodent and general pest treatment wherever activity is found during the survey
  • Termite and deemak inspection of wooden partitions, door frames and record rooms
  • Mosquito and dengue larvicide at roof tank surrounds, courtyards and blocked drains
  • Optional plastering and finish work bundled in, so patching, repair and cosmetic finish happen in the same mobilisation instead of chasing a separate thekedar afterwards
  • Fumigation certificate issued on completion, after a joint walkthrough of the building with you

Plastering and beautification are only sold inside the Full Building Package, as a bundled add on to treatment work. We do not take standalone plastering jobs.

Who we serve

Every Building Type Has a Different Pest Risk

The survey decides the plan, but the starting point is what the premises actually does all day. Six commercial types cover most of what we treat in Karachi.

🏢

Offices and corporate floors

Pantry ants, cockroaches out of the washroom stack, and rodents in false ceilings and cable trays where nobody looks. We treat after closing so Monday morning smells like an office, not a chemical store. Multi tenant buildings are treated floor by floor with a schedule the building manager can circulate in advance.

🍽

Restaurants and cafes

Kitchen drains and grease traps are where the cockroach problem actually lives, not the dining floor. Monthly cadence, night visits after the last order, and records you can show when the Sindh Food Authority inspector walks in. Full detail on the restaurant pest control Karachi page.

🏫

Schools and academies

Treatment goes in during term breaks and again after the monsoon, when courtyards, planters and water tanks start breeding dengue mosquitoes. Classrooms, canteens and hostel blocks each get their own pass. See school fumigation service Karachi.

🏭

Warehouses and godowns

Korangi, SITE, Landhi and the godowns around them all share one problem: rats in stacked stock and pallets nobody moves for months. Bait stations mapped to racking, perimeter and shutter gaps sealed, treatment logs kept for your auditors. See Korangi Industrial pest control.

🛍

Retail shops and plazas

In a plaza, shared drains and one garbage point mean one shop's infestation belongs to everyone. Treating a single shop rarely holds. We treat the plaza as a unit where the owners allow it, shutters down, after closing, so trading hours are untouched.

🏦

Banks and branch offices

Record rooms and file stores are a termite and silverfish risk long before anyone sees an insect on the counter. Server and UPS rooms attract rodents chewing cable. Discreet visits after banking hours, low odour treatment, and a certificate for the compliance file.

What it costs

Commercial Work Is Priced After the Survey, Not From a Rate Card

A single shop unit and a five floor building with a basement are not the same job, and no published table can honestly price both. So we do not publish one. Every commercial job and every annual maintenance contract is priced after the free site survey, and the number reaches you in writing.

What the survey settles: how many floors and zones, what pest pressure is actually present, how much access we get and when, whether the roof and basement are in scope, and whether finish work is bundled in. That is what moves the price, so that is what we look at before quoting.

If you want published, fixed prices for a home or a small single unit, those live on the Karachi price list. Commercial buildings do not fit there, and pretending otherwise wastes your time and ours.
