What is commercial cleaning in Karachi?

Commercial cleaning is a planned clean for a workplace, shop or shared building area. The useful starting point is a written scope: which rooms and surfaces are included, what access is available, when the work can happen and what needs a separate quote. Pest control or fumigation can be scoped alongside cleaning, with each service shown clearly.

Questions businesses ask

Commercial cleaning, explained plainly

What does commercial cleaning in Karachi include?

Commercial cleaning is scoped around the space rather than a single flat package. A quotation can cover work areas, floors, accessible glass, pantries, washrooms, high-touch surfaces and shared building areas, with the exact tasks and exclusions written down before scheduling.

Do you provide daily janitorial staff?

SafaiKaro quotes one-off and periodic deep cleans. If you need daily cleaning staff, share the hours and duties so availability and scope can be confirmed separately before booking.

How is a commercial cleaning quote prepared?

Share the building type, approximate area, location, floors or rooms, access window and the work you want done. SafaiKaro uses those details to clarify the scope, then confirms the included tasks, access assumptions and price in writing.

Can cleaning and pest control be requested together?

Yes. Cleaning and pest control are separate scopes, so the quotation should show each one clearly. For fumigation, termite, rodent or other pest work, see the commercial pest control page and mention the concern when you request a quotation.

Which areas of Karachi do you cover?

SafaiKaro serves businesses across Karachi, subject to the location, scope and scheduling details of the request. Share the broad area and building information on WhatsApp so the next step can be confirmed before a survey or quotation is arranged.

What should I send for a commercial cleaning quotation?

Send the broad area, building type, approximate size, number of floors or rooms, access hours, whether the work is one-off or recurring, and the surfaces or areas that need attention. Photos or a simple floor plan can help clarify the scope, but do not send confidential documents.
