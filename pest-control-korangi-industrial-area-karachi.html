Pest Control in Landhi (Residential) Karachi

Landhi is a combined residential and industrial municipality, bordered by Korangi Town to the north-west and Bin Qasim Port to the south and east. In several parts of Landhi, residential lanes back directly onto factory and warehouse boundaries — a shared boundary that lets stored-product pests and rodents move directly from commercial storage into adjoining homes.

River-adjacent, low-lying sections of Landhi also share the same Malir River flood exposure documented in Malir Cantt and Model Colony, with an added complication: industrial runoff and debris carried by floodwater. That combination of standing water and industrial-boundary rodent pressure makes Landhi's residential pockets a two-front pest environment rather than a single, isolated risk.

For homes near the industrial edge, we use entry-point sealing and tamper-resistant bait stations at boundary and utility points, the same approach we use for homes near any industrial or commercial edge elsewhere in Karachi. For flood-affected sections near the Malir River, larvicide is applied to standing water as part of the same visit, and entry-point sealing plus bait station placement covers both residential and industrial units affected by river flooding.

Residential fumigation in Landhi starts from Rs 7,000, rodent control from Rs 13,000 for homes up to 100 sq yards. Book an inspection if your home backs onto an industrial boundary or the river.
