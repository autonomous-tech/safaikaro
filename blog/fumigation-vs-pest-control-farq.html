Pehle Basics — Fumigation Kya Hai, Pest Control Kya Hai?

Pest Control — Broad Term

"Pest control" ek umbrella term hai jo har us method ko cover karta hai jo ghar ya building ke andar ya bahar pests (keeray, insects, rodents, termites) ko manage ya khatam kare. Isme shamil hai:

  • Chemical spray treatments (insecticides)
  • Gel baiting (cockroach gel, ant gel)
  • Termite soil injection ya baiting systems
  • Rodent traps aur bait stations
  • Mosquito fogging aur larvicide
  • Physical exclusion (entry points band karna)
  • Bed bug treatment (insecticide + IGR spray)

Yeh sab "pest control" ke andar aata hai.

Fumigation — Ek Specific Type

Fumigation pest control ka ek specific method hai — primarily liquid insecticide spray ya gas se ghar ke interior spaces mein insects ko khatam karna. Pakistan mein "fumigation" commonly refer karta hai liquid spray treatment — jo cockroaches, ants, silverfish, lizards, aur general household insects ke liye hoti hai. (Technical sense mein fumigation alag hai — tent fumigation ya gas fumigation — lekin common usage mein Karachi mein "ghar ki fumigation" mostly spray treatment hai.)

Simple rule: Fumigation pest control ka ek type hai. Jab koi "fumigation" maangta hai toh woh aksar general household insect spray treatment maang raha hota hai. Lekin termite, rodents, ya specific pests ke liye alag specialized method hoti hai.

Common Misconceptions — Yeh Galat Fehmiyan Door Karein

Misconception 1: "Fumigation se termite bhi khatam ho jaati hai"

Nahi. Spray fumigation ka chemical surface aur shallow soil mein jaata hai — termite colony underground hoti hai aur safe rehti hai. Termite treatment ke liye alag specialized method zaroori hai.

Misconception 2: "Annual fumigation se sab kuch cover ho jaata hai"

General annual fumigation cockroaches, ants, aur common insects cover karta hai — termite control, rodent management, aur bed bug treatment alag services hain. Annual plan mein kya kya included hai — SafaiKaro se confirm karein booking ke waqt.

Misconception 3: "Zyada chemical spray = zyada effective"

Galat. Pest control efficacy chemical concentration pe utni nahi jitnai correct formulation, correct application points, aur correct method pe depend karti hai. Yeh kyun hai ke DIY market sprays usually fail hote hain — concentration nahi, method aur formulation ka masla hota hai.

Misconception 4: "Fogging se mosquito breeding khatam ho jaati hai"

Fogging sirf adult mosquitoes ko kill karta hai — breeding sites (standing water) eliminate nahi karta. Dengue control ke liye fogging + larvicide + breeding source elimination teeno zaroori hain. Sirf fogging ek hafte mein repeat karni padti hai agar breeding sites still exist hain.
