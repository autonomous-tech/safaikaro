Fumigation Se Pehle Kya Karein — Ghar Tayar Karne Ka Complete Checklist

SafaiKaro Team June 2026 10 min read
Fumigation Prep Checklist Karachi Pest Control DHA Clifton

Fumigation book ho gayi — ab aage kya? Yeh sawal har customer ke zehan mein hota hai. Bahut log pehli baar pest control service le rahe hote hain aur unhe exactly nahi pata ke treatment se pehle kya karna hai, kya nahi karna, ghar se kitni der bahar rehna hai, aur treatment ke baad kab safe hai wapas jaana.

Yeh guide SafaiKaro ke technicians ne prepare ki hai — based on sab se aksar pooche jaane walay sawalat. Haan zaroori baat pehle: aapko bahut zyada kaam nahi karna. Sirf kuch specific cheezein hain jo treatment ko safe aur effective banane ke liye zaroori hain.

Time estimate: In tamam steps ke liye sirf 30–45 minute kaafi hain. Poori grihasti badlna zaroori nahi — sirf neeche diye hue points follow karein.

1. Khaana Aur Bartan — Kya Cover Karna Hai

Kitchen mein yeh sabse important step hai. Insecticide chemical airborne hota hai treatment ke waqt — khule khaane pe settle ho sakta hai. Isliye:

Kya Zaroor Cover Ya Band Karein

  • Khula khaana: Phal, sabziyaan, bread, biscuit tin, open snack packets — sab airtight containers ya sealed plastic bags mein band kar ke fridge mein rakhein.
  • Bacha hua pakka khaana: Salan, daal, chawal — fridge mein band dabbay mein.
  • Bartan, plates, glasses, cups: Agar kitchen cabinet mein hain aur cabinet band ho sakti hai — band kar dein. Agar khule shelf pe hain — plastic wrap ya newspaper se dhak dein.
  • Baby bottles aur feeding accessories: Sealed bag mein band karein — baad mein use se pehle dhulein.
  • Pet ka khaana: Pet food bag ya bowl bhi band kar dein.

Kya Cover Karna Zaroori Nahi

  • Band fridge ke andar rakhay khaane ko alag se cover karna zaroori nahi
  • Gas stove, microwave, ya fridge ki baahri surface
  • Sealed tin ya airtight packed khaana jo already closed ho

Simple rule: Agar khaana khula hai aur ek ghante mein use hona nahi — band karo. Doubt mein ho toh cover karo. Treatment ke baad open surfaces wipe karo before eating.

2. Pets — Unhe Kahan Rakhen Treatment Ke Din

Aapke pets ki safety bilkul zaroori hai. WHO-approved chemicals mammals ke liye drying ke baad safe hote hain — lekin treatment ke waqt direct exposure avoid karna chahiye.

Kuttay Aur Billiyan

Treatment ke din pets ko ghar se bahar le jaayein — neighbour ke yahaan, car mein (AC ke saath), ya kisi family member ke yahaan. Treatment complete hone ke baad technician aapko batayega — uske 4–6 ghante baad aur proper ventilation ke baad pets ko wapas la saktay hain.

Parinde (Birds)

Birds respiratory system bohot sensitive hota hai — pyrethroids unhe affect kar saktay hain zyada. Cage ko bahar rakhein — garden mein ya car mein. Treatment ke baad 6–8 ghante baad wapas laayein. Agar bahar le jana mumkin nahi toh cage ko damp cloth se completely cover karein aur door band kar dein.

Fish Tanks

Aquarium fish ke liye process alag hai: treatment se 2 ghante pehle tank ka aerator (air pump) band kar dein, aur tank ko plastic sheet se cover karein. Aerator band karna zaroori hai kyunki yeh airborne chemical ko paani mein pump kar sakta hai. Treatment ke baad cover hatao aur aerator wapas chalao — fish tank mein hawa wapas aani chahiye.

3. Indoor Plants — Treatment Se Pehle

Indoor plants ke pattay chemicals absorb karte hain — direct spray unhe harm kar sakta hai. Best option:

  • Indoor plants ko treatment ke din balcony pe ya bahar le jaayein (agar balcony treated area se alag ho)
  • Agar bahar nahi le ja saktay — plastic sheet se roots tak cover karein
  • Treatment ke 24 ghante baad plants wapas andar rakhein
  • Pattay pe agar chemical residue ho toh ek baar water spray se clean karein

Note: Garden ke outdoor plants pe treatment ka asar minimal hota hai — mostly indoor ya porch plants ki baat hai. Technician outdoor areas mein bhi spray karta hai depending on treatment type — garden plants usually safe hote hain.

4. Baki Ghar Ki Tayari — Room by Room

Bedroom

  • Bedsheets, pillow covers, duvet — treatment se pehle ya treatment ke din wash kar saktay hain (optional — technician access ke liye bed ke sides clear chahiye)
  • Bed ke neeche se juttay, bags, aur clutter hata dein — technician ko access chahiye
  • Wardrobe mein kapde normal tarah rakhein — band wardrobe ke andar clothes cover nahi hone chahiye treatment se
  • Makeup, perfumes, aur toiletries counter pe khuli hain toh ek side kar dein — yeh zaroori nahi cover karein, sirf access de dein

Bathroom

  • Toothbrush aur toothpaste glass mein hain? Band cabinet mein rakhein ya plastic bag mein
  • Soap aur shampoo khule hain toh ek drawer mein rakhein
  • Under-sink cabinet — pest control mein yeh areas target hote hain, isliye agar kuch rakhna hai toh nikalein aur baad mein wapas rakhein

Living Room

  • Sofa aur chairs ke sides aur neeche se clutter hataein — technician ko spray access chahiye
  • Children toys floor pe bikhre hain toh ek jagah uthaa dein
  • Books aur magazines shelves pe rakhe rehein — unhe move karna zaroori nahi

Quick Pre-Treatment Checklist (Print Ya Screenshot Karo)

  • Sab khula khaana fridge ya sealed containers mein
  • Bartan band cabinet mein ya covered
  • Baby bottles sealed
  • Pets bahar ya covered cage mein
  • Fish tank aerator band, tank covered
  • Indoor plants bahar ya covered
  • Bed ke neeche se clutter hata dein
  • Under-sink area clear
  • Toothbrush band drawer mein
  • Ghar se bahar jaane ki tayari (khud aur family)

6. Ghar Wapas Kab Jaayein — Re-Entry Guide

Yeh sabse aksar poochha jaane wala sawal hai. SafaiKaro ke WHO-approved chemical formulations ke liye yeh standard guidance hai:

  • Healthy adults: Treatment complete hone ke 2–3 ghante baad dwell time — phir doors aur windows 20–30 minute ke liye khol kar ventilate karein — phir andar aa saktay hain.
  • Bachche (3 saal se kam) aur neonates: 6–8 ghante baad, jab ghar fully ventilated ho.
  • Pregnant khawateen: 6–8 ghante — extra احتیاط ke taur pe.
  • Asthma ya respiratory conditions: Apne doctor se poochhein — aur technician ko pehle batayein taki appropriate formulation use ho.
  • Pets (dogs, cats): 4–6 ghante.
  • Birds: 6–8 ghante.

Ventilation step zaroori hai: Sirf waqt guzaarna kaafi nahi — ghar mein wapas aate waqt pehle khoob hawa aane dein (15–30 minute khidkiyan aur darvaze khol dein) phir permanently settle ho jaayein.

7. Treatment Ke Baad — After-Care Guide

Treatment ke baad kuch baatein follow karein taki treatment ka asar zyada waqt tak rahe:

Safai — Kya Kab Karein

  • Kitchen counter aur dining table: Re-entry ke baad wet cloth se wipe karein — khane ki surfaces pe khaana rakhne se pehle.
  • Floors: 24 ghante ke baad mop kar saktay hain — lekin corners aur skirting boards ko nahi dhona chahiye (residue wahan kaam karta hai).
  • Walls aur corners: Pehle 2–3 hafte tak scrub ya wipe mat karein — chemical residue ki wajah se late hatch hone waale insects bhi khatam hote hain.
  • Bedding: Re-entry ke din bedsheets change kar saktay hain — wash karein before next use agar spray direct hua hota.

Kya Expect Karein

  • Treatment ke 24–48 ghante mein insects zyada nazar aa saktay hain (dying insects nikalte hain) — yeh normal hai, ghabraayein nahi.
  • 72 ghante mein activity significantly kam ho jaani chahiye.
  • Agar 1 hafte baad bhi significant activity ho — SafaiKaro ko call karein (90-day guarantee ke under free re-treatment).
