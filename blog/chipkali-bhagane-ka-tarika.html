Chipkali Bhagane Ka Tarika: Pehle Samjhein Chipkali Aati Kyun Hai

Karachi ke gharon wali chipkali common house gecko hai (Hemidactylus frenatus aur Hemidactylus brookii). Yeh sirf zinda insects khaati hai: machar, patanga, makhi, cockroach ke bachche, makdi. Jahan khaana nahi, wahan chipkali nahi rukti. Chipkali ke liye koi pesticide bana hi nahi; hal raaste band karna aur khaana hatana hai.

  • Roshni: Raat ki tube light aur porch bulb udne wale insects kheenchte hain, chipkali unke peeche light ke paas baith jaati hai.
  • Insects ka stock: Kitchen ke cockroach, naali ki makhi, monsoon ke machar: chipkali ka roz ka khaana.
  • Khule raaste: Split AC ki pipe ka hole jo foam se poora band nahi, exhaust fan bina jaali, darwaze ke neeche ki gap.

July se September ke monsoon mein cockroach aur machar barhte hain, September se November machar ka peak hai, aur isi waqt chipkali ki shikayat sabse zyada hoti hai. Gulshan-e-Iqbal aur Gulistan-e-Johar ke flats mein seedhi ki light poori raat jalti hai, to chipkali darwaze ke paas jama hoti hai. DHA aur Clifton ke bungalows mein porch lights garden ke insects laati hain. Nazimabad aur PECHS ke purane gharon mein lakdi ke khirki frames aur khule roshandaan aam raasta hain.

Seedhi baat: Chaar paanch chipkali ka matlab insects ka masla pehle se hai. Chipkali symptom hai, insects bimari.

Chipkali Ko Bhagane Ka Tarika: Teen Qadam Jo Asal Mein Kaam Karte Hain

1. Insects khatam karein (sabse zaroori qadam)

Kitchen ke cockroach ke liye gel bait, deewaron aur skirting par residual spray. Jab khaana nahi bachta to chipkali 2 se 4 hafte mein khud bahar shift ho jaati hai. Market ka spray do din chalta hai, residual spray hafton. Cockroach spray ka alag guide hai: cockroach killer spray Karachi.

2. Gaps seal karein

Raat ko torch le kar dekhein chipkali kis raaste se aati hai. AC pipe ka hole silicone ya foam se band karein, exhaust fan par jaali, darwaze ke neeche rubber strip. Iske baghair nayi chipkali bahar se aa jaati hai.

3. Lights ka intezam

Bahar wali lights raat ko band rakhein, ya white ki jagah yellow bulb lagayein. Insects UV aur blue roshni ki taraf zyada aate hain, yellow light unhein kam kheenchti hai. Insect kam to chipkali kam.

Ek qadam kaafi nahi. Sirf spray ho aur gaps khule rahen to nayi chipkali aa jaati hai; sirf gaps band hon aur insects rahen to andar wali wahin rehti hai. Teeno saath: 3 se 6 hafte mein chipkali dikhna band ya bohat kam ho jaata hai.

Chipkali Ko Bhagane Ka Tarika In Urdu: Totkay Ka Sach

Urdu forums aur YouTube ke totkon mein ek hi kami hai: koi bhi chipkali ka khaana nahi hataata. Har ek ka asal asar:

Anday ke chilke

Khaali chilke kone mein rakhna ya chhat se latkana. Sookha chilka ek do din mein be-boo ho jaata hai aur chipkali static cheezon ki aadi jaldi ho jaati hai. Karachi ki humidity mein chilke par cheentiyan alag aati hain. Asar: koi nahi.

Mor pankh

Mor chipkali khaata hai, isliye pankh se darti hai, yeh khayal hai. Chipkali pankh ko pehchanti nahi, sirf harkat aur roshni par react karti hai. Asar: koi nahi.

Naphthalene balls (phenyl ki golian)

Tez boo chipkali ko ek band almari se kuch din door rakh sakti hai; Karachi ki garmi mein goli hafte bhar mein ghul jaati hai. Bachon ke liye zeher hai: medical case reports mein do golian nigalne se severe haemolytic anaemia hua hai. Asar: thoda, thodi der, risk ke saath.

Lehsan, pyaz aur kaali mirch ka spray

Boo jab tak taaza hai, chand ghante, chipkali us kone se hat sakti hai. Mirch ka paani geela ho to paon irritate karta hai, sookhte hi khatam. Chipkali doosre kamre mein jaati hai, ghar se nahi.

Coffee aur tambaku ki golian

Zeher ke tor par socha gaya totka. Chipkali sirf hilte hue zinda insect par jhapatti hai, rakhi hui goli ko chhooti nahi. Tambaku bachche ke haath lage to alag khatra. Asar: koi nahi.

Market ka chipkali spray, coil aur glue trap

Jo "lizard spray" asal mein insect killer hai woh isliye kaam karta hai ke chipkali ka khaana maarta hai. Boo wale spray aur coil naphthalene jaisa temporary asar dete hain. Glue trap ek chipkali pakadta hai, agli usi jagah aa jaati hai.

Verdict: Boo wale totkay chipkali ko ek kone se doosre kone mein shift karte hain. Jab tak machar, patanga aur cockroach ghar mein hain, chipkali wapas aati rahegi.
