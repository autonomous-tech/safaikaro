What is commercial kitchen cleaning in Karachi?

Commercial kitchen cleaning is a planned clean of a working kitchen, done while it is out of use. SafaiKaro cleans the room and the accessible equipment surfaces: floors, walls, splashbacks, the outside of cooking and refrigeration equipment, counters, shelving, sinks and the bin area. Hood canopies, filters and ducted exhaust are a separate specialist scope that we survey before we quote them.

This page is written for commercial kitchens in Karachi: canteens and hostel, office and staff dining kitchens. For the rest of the workplace, see office cleaning in Karachi.

Three groups, kept apart

What we price from your details and what we survey first

Kitchen work goes wrong when one line in a quote hides more than one job. We keep these three groups apart from your first message, so nobody finds a gap on the day of the clean.

01

Room and equipment surfaces

We price this from your kitchen details and photos.

  • Floors, skirting and floor drain covers
  • Walls, splashbacks and tiled surfaces
  • Outside surfaces of ranges, fryers, ovens, griddles and refrigeration units

  • Sinks, washing areas and the bin area

Hood, filter and exhaust scope

We survey these before we quote them.

  • Whether our own team or a specialist subcontractor does each item, named in your written quote
03

Cleaning is not pest treatment

A clean is not a pest treatment, but pest control for the kitchen can be quoted alongside the cleaning. See pest control for businesses.

  • Taking grease and soil off surfaces removes much of what attracts cockroaches, rodents and flies
  • It does not treat activity that is already there, and a clean is not a treatment
  • Tell us what you have seen, so it goes into the cleaning notes instead of getting cleaned over

What changes the price

Kitchen details that move the quote

SafaiKaro does not quote kitchen work from a rate card. We build the quotation from what you send and what the survey finds, and these are the items that move it most.

  • Grease build upA kitchen cleaned monthly and one that has run a year without a clean are not the same job.
  • Equipment countHow many cooking, holding and refrigeration units there are, what type they are, and whether your staff can move them.
  • Working windowHow many hours you can keep the kitchen out of use, and when. The schedule is agreed at the survey or before booking.
  • AccessFloor level, lift availability for equipment, water pressure, drain fall, and a power point that will run cleaning machines. Tell us if the kitchen runs on tanker water, or on a generator during a KE outage.
  • Specialist itemsWhether you want hood, filter or exhaust work looked at, and whether it can be done at your site at all.
  • Repeat patternA one off reset, or a recurring visit on a pattern we agree with you.

Kitchen cleaning, answered plainly

What does commercial kitchen cleaning in Karachi include?

SafaiKaro cleans the kitchen room and the accessible equipment surfaces: floors and floor drain covers, walls and splashbacks, the outside of ranges, fryers, ovens, griddles and refrigeration units, counters, shelving, storage racks, sinks, washing areas and the bin area. Hood canopies, filters and ducted exhaust are a separate specialist scope that we survey before we quote them.

Do you clean kitchen hoods, filters and exhaust ducts?

SafaiKaro does not include hood, filter or exhaust duct cleaning automatically in a commercial kitchen quote, and we do not clean inside ducts. What else we can take depends on the hood type and whether your kitchen staff can lift the filters out safely. We survey the hood, the duct route and where the extract fan sits, and the written quotation names whether our own team or a specialist subcontractor does each item.

What is excluded from a commercial kitchen clean?

Grease traps and liquid waste, the inside of ducted exhaust runs, taking equipment apart or putting it back together, gas and electrical work, repairs, and anything needing height access. Pest control is not part of the cleaning price, though it can be quoted alongside the cleaning. SafaiKaro issues no hygiene certificate, audit result or inspection sign off. We write every exclusion into the quotation before the visit.

When can a commercial kitchen be cleaned?

The kitchen has to be out of use while we work: counters cleared, equipment cool, and nobody walking on the floor while we wash and rinse it. Tell us the hours the kitchen can be out of use. The schedule is agreed at the survey, or before booking when surface work is quoted from photos, and the working window goes in writing.

How is a commercial kitchen cleaning quote prepared?

Send the broad area, the approximate kitchen size, the main equipment, the hours the kitchen can be out of use and whether you want hood or exhaust work looked at. The written quotation then lists the tasks, the access assumptions and the exclusions.
