Office cleaning questions

Know the scope before you book

What is included in office cleaning in Karachi?

A quote can cover office work areas, floors, accessible glass, meeting spaces, washrooms, pantries, high-touch surfaces and shared areas. The included tasks and exclusions are written around the office layout and requested scope.

Do you provide daily office janitorial staff?

SafaiKaro quotes one-off and periodic deep cleans. If you need daily cleaning staff, share the hours and duties so availability and scope can be confirmed separately before booking.

What details are needed for an office cleaning quote?

Send the broad area, approximate office size, number of floors or rooms, access window, timing and the surfaces or rooms that need attention. Non-confidential photos or a simple floor plan can help clarify the scope.

Can office cleaning and pest control be quoted together?

Yes. Cleaning and pest control are separate scopes. If you need fumigation, termite, rodent or cockroach work, mention it in the enquiry so the quotation can show each service clearly.

Which Karachi areas do you serve for office cleaning?

SafaiKaro serves offices across Karachi, subject to the location, scope and scheduling details of the request. Share the broad area and building information before a survey or quotation is arranged.

Do you clean office carpets or upholstery?

Carpet or upholstery work can be considered as a separate quoted scope. Confirmation depends on the fabric, condition, area, drying and access requirements, and any spot treatment details shared before booking.
