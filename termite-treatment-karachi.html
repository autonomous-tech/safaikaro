How to arrange treatment

  1. Send your Karachi area, property size and where you have seen termite activity.
  2. Confirm the treatment scope, price and available appointment with the team.
  3. Follow the preparation and return-home instructions provided for your treatment.

90-day guarantee

If termites return within 90 days of treatment, contact SafaiKaro for re-treatment at no additional cost. Confirm the areas covered by your treatment before work starts.
