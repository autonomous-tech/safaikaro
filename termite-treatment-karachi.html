Termite fumigation in Karachi: kya hota hai

Log isse "termite fumigation" kehte hain, lekin deemak (termite, دیمک) ke liye gas ya fog wali fumigation nahi hoti. Fumigation ka matlab hai band jagah mein gas chhodna. Spray ka matlab hai surface par dawa. Deemak ka asal treatment drill-and-inject hai, kyunki colony zameen ke andar rehti hai, lakdi mein nahi.

Karachi ke gharon mein jo deemak milti hai woh subterranean hai: Coptotermes aur Odontotermes species, jo mitti mein colony banati hain aur slab ki daraaron, expansion joints aur pipe ke suraakh se upar aati hain. Aap ko deewar par mitti ki naali (mud tube) ya darwaze ke frame mein khokhli lakdi nazar aati hai. Queen aur colony neeche hoti hai.

Is liye treatment ka target soil hai. Technician wall-floor junction ke saath har 12 inch par lagbhag 12 mm ka hole drill karta hai, us mein non-repellent termiticide inject karta hai, aur phir darwaze, chaukhat, kitchen cabinets aur almari ki infested lakdi par surface treatment karta hai. Holes usi din cement se seal ho jaate hain.

Deemak ke liye dawai: kaunsi aur kitni der ke liye door rehna hai

Hum non-repellent termiticide use karte hain, fipronil ya imidacloprid class ki (phenylpyrazole ya neonicotinoid). Non-repellent ka matlab: deemak ko dawa mehsoos nahi hoti, woh treated soil se guzarti hai aur dawa apne saath colony tak le jaati hai. Purane repellent sprays (pyrethroid, jaise bifenthrin) deemak ko sirf raasta badalne par majboor karte hain; colony wahin rehti hai.

Yeh dawa be-boo nahi hoti; halki chemical smell ventilation se nikal jaati hai. Koi bhi termiticide 100 percent risk-free nahi hota, is liye treated kamron mein bachon aur pets ko 4 ghante tak na jaane dein, jab tak injected points aur treated lakdi sookh na jaye. Baad mein farsh par bacha hua koi daagh geele kapde se saaf kar dein.

Aap ki taiyari: furniture deewar se ek foot hata dein, almari aur kitchen cabinets ka neechla khaana khaali kar dein, aur kitchen ka khula khaana dhak dein. Technician ko har deewar ka neechla hissa nazar aana chahiye.

Deemak ka ilaj ghar par: what DIY gets you

Market ka deemak spray, kerosene ya neem oil lakdi ki surface par nazar aane wale workers ko maar deta hai. Colony zameen mein hai; surface spray se us par koi asar nahi hota, aur kuch hafton mein naye workers wahi mud tube dobara bana lete hain.

Boric acid lakdi par lagane se woh lakdi kuch der ke liye kam khaayi jaati hai, lekin deemak saath wali chaukhat par chali jaati hai. Almari ko dhoop mein rakhna sirf us almari ka masla hal karta hai, deewar ka nahi.

DIY ka ek faida hai: pehchaan. Agar aap ne chaukhat par tap karke khokhli awaaz suni ya deewar par mitti ki naali dekh li, toh aap ne masla sahi waqt par pakad liya hai. Har desi nuskhe ka jaiza aur deemak ki pehchaan ke paanch signs deemak ka ilaj post mein hain; yeh page us ke baad ka qadam hai, yani service aur price.
