Pest Control in Korangi Town Karachi

Korangi Town is a large, dense residential district bordering the Korangi Industrial Area, with a mix of older row-housing and newer apartment construction. Its proximity to industrial godowns and warehousing on the district's edge is a documented rodent-migration route into the adjoining residential lanes — rats moving out of storage facilities follow drainage and utility lines directly into nearby homes.

Korangi Road connects the town directly to Landhi and the Korangi Industrial Area, and the heavy through-traffic and commercial frontage along this corridor concentrate food-service outlets that sustain cockroach populations spreading into the residential side streets behind them. In Korangi Town's apartment buildings, that same cockroach pressure also moves laterally through shared risers and inter-unit gaps, the same dynamic documented in DHA Phase 5.

Our rodent programme for Korangi Town prioritises entry-point sealing and tamper-resistant bait stations at utility and boundary points near the industrial edge, since blocking the entry route is more durable than baiting alone in an area with ongoing external pressure. For apartment buildings, we offer block-rate pricing when 4 or more units in the same building book fumigation together.

General fumigation from Rs 7,000, rodent control from Rs 13,000 for homes up to 100 sq yards. Ask about block-rate pricing if you live in a Korangi Town apartment building.
