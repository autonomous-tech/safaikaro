Pest Control in Bahadurabad Karachi

Bahadurabad is a dense, affluent commercial-residential hub centred on Bahadurabad Chowrangi, with a mix of older bungalows and taller apartment and plaza construction. Commercial ground floors along the Chowrangi generate steady food waste that sustains cockroach populations, which feed into the residential floors directly above them.

High-density apartment construction along Bahadurabad's main roads means shared risers and lift shafts are the primary route pests use to move between units — the same pattern documented in DHA Phase 5 and Gulistan-e-Johar. A single-flat treatment in one of these buildings is quickly reinfested from neighbouring units through the shared riser.

Our Bahadurabad protocol pairs perimeter gel baiting and drainage cover treatment at ground-floor commercial entry points with building-wide fumigation for apartment blocks. We offer block-rate pricing when 4 or more units in the same building book together.

General fumigation in Bahadurabad starts from Rs 7,000 for residential units up to 100 sq yards; commercial rates are available for plaza and restaurant tenants around the Chowrangi. See our commercial service.

Pest Control in Karsaz Karachi

Karsaz sits directly on Shahrah-e-Faisal, one of the specific locations repeatedly named in Karachi monsoon flooding reports alongside Teen Talwar, Do Talwar, and Bath Island. Recurring standing water along Karsaz Road after heavy rain creates a reliable mosquito-breeding cycle that residents deal with every monsoon season.

Karsaz is also home to Naval Housing Scheme (NHS Karsaz), Askari 4, and other defence housing schemes with larger individual plots and garden landscaping. Garden timber and ornamental features in these schemes create the same termite-stepping-stone risk documented in DHA Phase 6's villa belt — termites move from soil into garden furniture and planters before reaching the main structure.

For Karsaz's flooding exposure, we apply WHO-approved larvicide to all standing water points and residual insecticide to shaded wall surfaces as part of every fumigation visit, reducing adult mosquito populations by over 90% within 72 hours. For NHS Karsaz and Askari 4's garden termite risk, we cover the full property perimeter, garden structures included, not just the house.

General fumigation with larvicide included starts from Rs 7,000, and full-perimeter termite proofing for larger Karsaz properties is priced from Rs 17,000. Book ahead of monsoon season for the best availability.

Pest Control in Nursery & Baloch Colony Karachi

Pest Control on Bath Island Karachi
